About AI Health Assistant

Informational risk insights powered by trained machine-learning models — built to help you understand patterns, not to replace your doctor.

🩸 Diabetes Model

An XGBoost classifier trained on demographic, metabolic and lifestyle indicators such as HbA1c and blood glucose level.

❤️ Heart Disease Model

An XGBoost classifier trained on clinical vitals including blood pressure, cholesterol, ECG and exercise response.

💊 Treatment-Need Model

A classifier trained on workplace and mental-health survey data to gauge whether treatment may be indicated.

This AI Health Assistant provides informational insights and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
